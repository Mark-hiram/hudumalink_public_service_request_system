WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help centre

Quick guidance for using HudumaLink.

＋

How do I report an issue?

Select “Report an issue”, choose the most relevant category, add a location and clear description, then submit. A tracking number will be created.

⌕

How do I track my report?

Open “Track a request” and enter the tracking number you received after submission.

◷

What do the statuses mean?

Pending: awaiting review. In Progress: being worked on. Resolved: marked as completed.

♙

Is this an official government service?

No. HudumaLink is a demonstration project for a public service request system and is not an official government website.
